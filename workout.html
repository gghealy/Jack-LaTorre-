How to use this

This is an exercise library, not a personalised training prescription. Start gently, use the movement shown in the video, and ask a parent, coach or qualified exercise professional to check unfamiliar movements. Stop if anything hurts. Don’t try to copy advanced gym loading from a video.

How Jack describes his training approach

Jack’s public material emphasises mobility, strength, suppleness, flexibility and body awareness—not simply doing more repetitions. In a podcast interview he specifically mentions seated good mornings, butterfly, elephant walks and heel slides/heel taps as useful examples. He also says to choose movements that address the rider’s own limitations rather than assume one routine suits everyone.

Because you’re still growing, I’ve kept this as a video library and avoided prescribing heavy weights or a hard daily schedule.
